# Second Unit — 3-minute trailer script

**Title card (3s):** SECOND UNIT — Autonomous Studio Crew  
**Subtitle:** Brief → rights-cleared cut

### Beat 1 — The problem (20s)
VO: “Marketing needs a 20-second reel. The archive has thousands of clips. Rights? Unknown. Approval? Email chains. Four weeks.”
B-roll: cluttered shared drive, red “license unknown” stickers.

### Beat 2 — Brief in (25s)
Show dashboard. Type brief. Director narrates: “Researcher is pulling candidates.”
Thumbnails stream in from Partner Archive MCP.

### Beat 3 — Edit (20s)
Editor sequences cut. EDL + captions appear. “Rough cut ready.”

### Beat 4 — Clearance moat (35s)
Clearance Officer flags 2 clips red. Calls Rights MCP. Swaps to cleared alts. Report goes green.
On-screen: `check_clip_rights` → `find_cleared_alternative`.

### Beat 5 — Studio Head gate (40s)
Banner: Awaiting Releasing Producer sign-off.
Marketing clicks Approve → **DENIED BY IAM** (flash red).
Switch principal → Releasing Producer → Approve (green).

### Beat 6 — Delivery + audit (25s)
Distributor publishes package. Release URI + clearance report.
Audit trail scrolls.  
**End card:** “Brief to rights-cleared cut in minutes, not weeks.”  
**Built with Gemini + Google Cloud ADK + Partner Archive MCP + Rights MCP**
